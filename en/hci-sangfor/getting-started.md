# Getting Started with HCI Cloud Platform

Follow the steps below to access and start using the HCI Cloud Platform.

---

## Step 1 — Open the console

Open your browser and go to the HCI platform address provided by your administrator:

```
https://HCI_IP
```

You will see the Sangfor Cloud Platform login page.

> **Supported browsers:** Chrome 23+, Firefox 18+, IE 10+, Safari 8+.

---

## Step 2 — Log in

Enter your **Username** or **Email** and **Password**, check the **Terms of Use** checkbox, then click **Log In**.

> If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password?** to recover it via the email address linked to your account.

After a successful login, you are taken to the **Overview** dashboard.

---

## Step 3 — Explore the console

The top navigation bar gives you access to all platform sections:

| Section | What's here |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Resource overview — VM count, alerts, pending work orders, and elastic IP status |
| **Compute** | Create and manage virtual machines and images |
| **Networking** | VPC topology, subnets, elastic IPs, access control, and port mapping |
| **Security Services** | Distributed firewall policies, virtual firewall (vNGAF), and SSL VPN |
| **Reliability** | VM backup policies, disaster recovery, and auto scaling |
| **Monitor Center** | Performance metrics, alerts, and resource quota usage |
| **Management** | Recycle bin for deleted resources |
| **Bills** | View your expense records |
| **Work Orders** | Request additional resource quotas from your administrator |
| **Users and Access Management** | Manage sub-accounts within your tenant |

---

## Done

You are now in the HCI Cloud Platform console. To get started:

- [Create your first virtual machine](console-guide/virtual-machines.md)
- [Set up networking](console-guide/networking.md)
- [Configure security policies](console-guide/security.md)

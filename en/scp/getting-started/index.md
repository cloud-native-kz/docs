# Getting Started with the SCP

The Self-Service Cloud Portal (SCP) is your tenant workspace on the HCI platform. It lets you request and manage virtual machines and track alerts.

This guide walks you through getting access, logging in, and orienting yourself in the portal.

## 1. How Access Works

Access to the SCP is **provided by your administrator**. There is no open self-registration.

Before you can log in, your administrator must:

1. Create a **tenant account** for you on the HCI platform.
2. Set an initial password (or provide you with a temporary one).
3. Define the resources available to your tenant at the platform level.

VM requests you submit through the portal go through administrator approval before any VM is provisioned.

You will receive the portal URL and your credentials directly from your administrator or account manager.

> **Haven't received your credentials?** Contact your account manager or email [cns-support@fcd.kz](mailto:cns-support@fcd.kz).

## 2. Logging In

Open the portal URL provided by your administrator in a web browser. The login page is at:

```
https://<SCP-address>/login
```

<!-- screenshot: SCP login page -->

**Steps:**

1. Enter your **username or email** in the Username/Email field.
2. Enter your **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

After successful authentication you will be taken to the portal, landing on the **Virtual Machines** page.

> **Note:** Use a modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, or Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

---

## 3. Resetting Your Password

**If you have forgotten your password:** on the login page, click the **Forgot Password?** link to the right of the Password field and follow the on-screen instructions.

<!-- screenshot: SCP login page — Forgot Password link -->

**To change your password while logged in:** click your username in the top-right corner of the screen (displayed as **Username / Role**, for example *Zhanat / Member*) to open the account dropdown, then select **Change Password**.

<!-- screenshot: SCP account dropdown -->

---

## 4. First Login Overview

After logging in you will land on the **Virtual Machines** page — the main working area of the portal.

<!-- screenshot: SCP Virtual Machines page — first login, no VMs yet -->

From here you can request your first VM using the **+ Request** button, or use the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left corner to navigate to other sections.

> **Ready to request your first VM?** See [First Steps](./first-steps.md).
